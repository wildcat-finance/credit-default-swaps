# Lender brief

You choose the amount and entry time. Your premium falls as expiry approaches, but the quoted annual
spread does not change. Entry closes when market grace plus 90 days remains. To buy, you tender
canonical wrapper shares representing the attached debt.

What you hold is not a detached insurance token. The receipt carries the wrapper debt and fixed cover
together. You can transfer it, hold it to settlement or burn early to recover the wrapper shares.
Early unprotection does not refund premium.

Your main operational duty is checkpoint and claim monitoring. A qualifying default must be recorded
no later than expiry, and cash claims end one year later. The prototype supplies no keeper or frontend.
