# Brand and visuals

The artwork follows the supplied Wildcat brand guide: Bunker `#141414`, Ultramarine `#3E68FF`,
Purple Heart `#4D26BC`, Light Slate `#7D71FF` and restrained Carmine `#F1464B`.

All three assets are generated PNG files at 1920 by 1080. They contain no text or logos, so exact
product claims remain in reviewable Markdown. No SVG substitute is authored.

- `covered-debt-hero.png`: one protected position
- `continuous-cover-lifecycle.png`: staggered fills, unused capacity and terminal paths
- `default-and-maturity.png`: default and healthy settlement fork
